About ChefLens AI

ChefLens AI is a Chrome extension that turns chaotic YouTube cooking videos into clean, actionable recipes directly inside your browser side panel. No more scrubbing timelines or writing down ingredients by hand.

Core Features

  • Instant Recipe Extraction: Automatically gathers full ingredient lists and step-by-step instructions with clickable video timestamps.
  • Smart Unit & Servings Scaler: Instantly switch between Metric (grams/ml) and US Imperial (cups/tbsp), or scale portion sizes dynamically.
  • Cookware & Substitution Pro Tips: Identifies necessary kitchen equipment (Dutch ovens, cast iron, etc.) and provides tested substitute guidance to prevent burning and sticking.
  • One-Click Grocery & Gear Sourcing: Quick search links to source missing spices, fresh produce, and recommended cookware on Amazon and grocery delivery partners.
